Project Synopsis & Engineering Specification

Otterlook AI: Personal Colour Analysis & Recommendation System

AI/ML-Driven Dermatological Colorimetry & Precision Styling Engine

Computer Vision & Machine Learning FastAPI & OpenCV & Scikit-Learn MediaPipe FaceMesh

1. Project Idea

Otterlook AI is an end-to-end computer vision and dermatological colorimetry platform that analyzes facial portraits, isolates anatomically stable skin regions, extracts multi-space colorimetric feature moments, predicts biological skin undertones (Warm, Cool, or Neutral) using machine learning classifiers, and generates tailored styling palettes for wardrobe, cosmetics, accessories, and seasonal harmonies.

2. Problem Statement

1. Subjectivity & Prohibitive Cost of In-Person Draping: Conventional personal color consultation relies on manual fabric draping by human stylists, which is expensive, geographically inaccessible, subjective, and highly prone to ambient lighting variations and human bias.
2. Failure Across Diverse Skin Phototypes: Existing automated smartphone apps and consumer camera filters sample single random pixels or enforce hardcoded RGB threshold rules. These approaches fail catastrophically across global skin diversity (Fitzpatrick types I–VI).
3. Contamination by Non-Skin Artifacts: Naive facial sampling without anatomical landmark isolation gets distorted by facial hair, cosmetics, lips, eyes, specular camera flash highlights, and shadows, leading to inaccurate undertone predictions and clashing wardrobe suggestions.

3. Proposed AI/ML & CS Solution

  • Automated Quality Control (QC): Image validation verifying dimensions, exposure/contrast histogram clipping, and blur using the Laplacian variance operator before execution.
  • Anatomical Multi-Region Landmark Segmentation: Employs MediaPipe FaceMesh (468 landmarks) with OpenCV Haar cascade fallback to precisely isolate 4 stable skin regions (Forehead, Left Cheek, Right Cheek, Jaw/Chin) while eliminating lips, eyes, eyebrows, and hair.
  • Dual-Space Glare & Outlier Rejection: Combines YCrCb ($Cr \in [128, 178], Cb \in [75, 135]$) and HSV skin masks with statistical Interquartile Range (IQR) luminance trimming (removing upper/lower 20% tails) to reject specular flash glare and cast shadows.
  • Multi-Space Dermatological Colorimetry: Extracts statistical moments across sRGB, HSV, and CIELAB ($L^*a^*b^*$), deriving the Individual Typology Angle ($\text{ITA}^\circ$) and the yellow-to-erythema chromatic ratio ($b^*/a^*$).
  • Supervised Ensemble Inference: Deploys a trained RandomForestClassifier pipeline to generate calibrated classification probabilities across Warm, Cool, and Neutral classes.
  • Expert Knowledge Recommendation Engine: Generates tailored styling profiles including core wardrobe colors, foundation/lipstick cosmetic guidelines, jewelry metal compatibilities (Gold vs. Silver), and clashing colors to avoid.
  • Privacy-First & Low-Latency API: Asynchronous FastAPI service processing images strictly in-memory (RAM) with no persistent facial storage.

4. Key Modules

Module 1: Image Quality Assessment (IQA)

Checks resolution, exposure/contrast clipping, and computes Laplacian variance to reject blurry or poorly lit portrait uploads.

Module 2: Facial Landmark & Anatomical ROI Segmenter

Uses 468-point MediaPipe FaceMesh to construct sub-orbital cheek patches, forehead band, and mentalis jawline region.

Module 3: Glare, Shadow & Outlier Filter

Executes joint YCrCb/HSV skin masks coupled with 20% IQR luminance trimming to isolate pristine, unadulterated skin pixels.

Module 4: Colorimetric Feature Extraction Engine

Computes 20+ numerical descriptors across sRGB, HSV, CIELAB ($L^*, a^*, b^*$), $\text{ITA}^\circ$, and chromatic ratios ($b^*/a^*, R/G, R/B$).

Module 5: ML Undertone Classification Engine

StandardScaler normalized Random Forest ensemble predicting Warm, Cool, or Neutral undertone with class probabilities.

Module 6: Styling & Palette Recommendation Engine

Knowledge-based mapping connecting undertone and 4-season subtypes to wardrobe swatches, cosmetics, jewelry, and colors to avoid.

5. Algorithms / Techniques

Domain / Step Algorithms / Methodologies Operational Role & Impact
Facial Landmark Mesh MediaPipe FaceMesh (468 3D points) + OpenCV Haar Cascade fallback Sub-millimeter anatomical skin patch localization without feature overlap.
Image Quality & Glare Filtering Laplacian Variance + YCrCb/HSV Thresholds + IQR Luminance Trimming Rejects blurry images and purges flash glare, hot spots, and shadow noise.
Dermatological Colorimetry CIELAB ($L^*a^*b^*$) & $\text{ITA}^\circ = \arctan((L^*-50)/b^*) \times (180/\pi)$ Quantifies biological melanin and yellow/red balance independently of ambient lightness.
Machine Learning Classifier Random Forest Classifier (150 trees, max depth 10, balanced) Non-linear decision boundaries yielding 90.74% test accuracy and 0.9075 Macro F1.
Comparative Baselines Support Vector Machines (Linear & RBF), Multinomial Logistic Regression Benchmarked across 5-fold stratified cross-validation for validation rigor.

6. Datasets

  • Personal Color Face Dataset: Asian/Korean 4-season portrait facial images labelled with seasonal color classifications (Spring Warm, Autumn Warm, Summer Cool, Winter Cool) for real-world facial validation.
  • The Pudding / TidyTuesday Global Complexion & Undertone Dataset: 6,800+ commercial foundation formulations spanning diverse global skin tones (Fitzpatrick I–VI) and labeled undertones (Warm, Cool, Neutral).
  • Google Monk Skin Tone (MST) Scale & Fitzpatrick Reference Index: 10-shade inclusive dermatological benchmark scale used for colorimetric calibration across equitable demographic phototypes.
  • Derived Feature Matrix (data/training.csv): Extracted numerical dataset combining multi-space color moments ($sRGB, HSV, L^*a^*b^*$, $\text{ITA}^\circ$, and ratios) mapped to ground-truth undertone labels.

Objective Undertone Classification

Accurate determination of Warm, Cool, or Neutral undertone with calibrated class probabilities (>90% accuracy).

Dermatological Color Profile

Personalized Styling Palettes

Curated clothing swatches, cosmetic foundation/lip/blush recommendations, metals (Gold vs. Silver), and clashing colors to avoid.

Interactive Visual Feedback & Privacy

Live portrait preview with Canvas ROI patch overlays; sub-1.5s latency with zero persistent facial storage in RAM.
